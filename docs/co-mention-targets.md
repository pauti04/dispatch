# Co-mention targets — Dispatch · Tech

A non-code asset. List of newsletters / publications whose audience overlaps with
Dispatch's. For each: contact, what we'd offer, draft outreach.

The goal is NOT free promotion. It's reciprocal mention — they tell their readers
about us once, we tell ours about them once, both audiences grow. Founders of small
publications generally welcome this; it's audience routing, not advertising.

---

## Tier 1 — closest audience overlap

### 1. Refactoring (Luca Rossi)
- **Audience:** ~70k working engineers, managers
- **Overlap:** ~90% — both serve working developers, both editorial in tone
- **Offer:** Dispatch features Refactoring in a "By the Editor" recurring column once a
  quarter; Refactoring mentions Dispatch in a "tools the editor uses" line
- **Draft DM:**
  > Hi Luca — I run Dispatch (https://dispatch.local), a daily 5-min brief for working
  > developers. Audience-wise we're basically twins. Would you be open to a one-time
  > cross-mention? You point to one Dispatch issue, we point to one Refactoring piece in
  > our weekly column. No financial exchange — just audience routing. Happy to draft the
  > copy and let you edit.

## Outreach cadence

- Week 1 after launch: pitch Tier 1 (4 emails, personalized, no template)
- Week 2: pitch Tier 2 (6 emails, lighter touch, template OK)
- Week 3: pitch Tier 3 (5 emails, opportunistic)

- One pitch per founder. If no reply in 7 days, send ONE polite nudge then drop it.
- Track every send + reply in a spreadsheet.
- If a cross-mention happens, do the reciprocal piece WITHIN the agreed week —
  never miss your side of the trade.

## What NOT to do

- Don't buy paid placement until there's revenue
- Don't pitch a publication whose audience won't care — your "no" rate gets noted
- Don't follow up more than once — founders read everything; a second nudge is rude
- Don't mass-template Tier 1 — they will recognize it and respond accordingly
- Don't promise our audience to anyone — we work for them, not for advertisers
